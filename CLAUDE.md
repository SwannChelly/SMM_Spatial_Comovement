# SMM Spatial Comovement — Codebase Guide

The Julia code estimates ONE specification: granular varieties (finite `N_s`),
comparative advantage at the attraction-area level with `T_ls = T_{a(l)s} · n_ls^κ`,
the cell-level cloglog extensive margin (block 4 = `[β_d ; β_κ]`), and ONE estimation
mode: the PSO searches (α, κ) only, and T with the aggregates (χ^L, χ^s, A) is profiled
out of the search by a joint GE-Sinkhorn inversion (`profile_ge`), with one foreign competitor
per sector (Φ^F_s, a column of T, profiled with it on the import share). The word "head" is
retired: use "aggregates" for (χ^L, χ^s, A); the aggregate shares are written χ (not Ω),
and Julia CODE uses roman identifiers only (`alpha`, `kappa`, `chi_L`, `chi_s`, ...); Greek
letters stay in comments and docstrings. The previous multi-specification code (GMM,
continuum, ZE-level CA, LPM, firm-level regression, CMA-ES/TikTak, joint T search) is
frozen in `old_version/`, with its own `CLAUDE.md` and full changelog. Do not add
backward compatibility to the root code; restore from `old_version/` if needed.

The Python reporting (`utils.py`, `report_lib.py`, `diffusion_lib.py`, `granular_lib.py`,
the notebooks, `test/*.py`) is not part of this rewrite and must not be changed by Julia
work. Two contracts tie them together: `load_parameters.jl` must keep the line
`@everywhere const theta = $(…)` (parsed by `utils._theta_from_julia`), and the artefact
names/locations below must not move.

## Model objects

Parameter vector (levels): `[χ^L(1) | χ^s(S) | A(R_downstream) | α(N_TAU) | κ(1) | T(sum(T_MASK))]`,
the T block including Φ^F_s (column `FOREIGN_COL`) for every importing sector.
χ^s is normalised to sum to 1, A to A[1] = 1, T per sector to `T[s, ref] = 1`.
Only (α, κ) are searched. The objective replaces the aggregates (χ^L, χ^s, A) and the T
block with their profile at (α, κ) (`profile_theta`), which matches γ and blocks 1–3
exactly; a particle whose profile does not converge gets `Inf` (never scored at a
fabricated T). The loss is blocks 4–6 in Step 1 and block 4 in Step 3.

Moment vector (masked): `[labor | industry (S−1) | π_r (R_d−1) | β_d (N_REG) ; β_κ | γ_{s,a} (active, non-ref) | Ḡ_s(0) (S)]`,
`BLOCK_RANGES` a 6-tuple. Assemble with `moments_to_vec`, never by hand.

Two index spaces: CELLS (`CELL_MASK`, `n_good`, `GOOD_S/R`, draw columns `1:n_good`, then
one foreign draw column per sector; control cells included) and T COLUMNS = attraction
areas + the foreign column (`T_COL_DIM = n_AA + 1`, `FOREIGN_COL`, `T_MASK`, `SECTOR_T_COLS`
with it, `SECTOR_DOM_COLS` without, `EMP_GAMMA_T` = shares of total purchases). `T[s,l] = T_area[s, AA_OF_ZE[l]] · n_ls^κ` (`gather_T_to_ze`).

### Invariant: s-major T flattening
The reduced T block and the γ moment block enumerate (sector, area) pairs in the same
order — all areas of sector 1, then sector 2 — so the T[s,a] parameter ↔ γ[s,a] moment.
`T_MASK = vec(permutedims(T_ACTIVE))`; flat position `(s-1)·T_COL_DIM + a`.
`T_ID_REDUCED` drops each sector's reference area and aligns with the γ moments.

### Weighting and inference: fake worlds
W lives on block 4 (`[β_d ; β_κ]`, `BLOCK_RANGES[4]` order) only; γ, blocks 1–3 and G are
reproduced exactly by the profile and the N bisection. No data covariance is read. In
every world, T and the aggregates are re-profiled on the world's own moments (`profile_world`).

## Caveats

- Read T identification per sector: the T block of `J'WJ` is block-diagonal by sector,
  and the global λ_min mixes sectors through a units/weighting artefact.
- Block 4 averages β̂ over `R_CELL_REG = 100` nested networks of N̂_s varieties; failed
  fits are excluded and counted (`reg_fail`); all failed ⇒ NaN ⇒ the particle is rejected.
